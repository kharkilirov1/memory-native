## Rescale asymmetry: root cause and fix (CPU investigation, not a G4 measurement)

Root cause: `PackedNA` rounded `w / s_new` with `torch.bucketize(a, mids)` (default
`right=False`), which sends an **exact midpoint tie to the lower rung, i.e. towards -inf**.
After a refresh that moves the scale one octave up, every ±0.5·s rung lands exactly on the
0 / 0.5 midpoint (±0.25), because scales are powers of two. So +0.5 → 0 but -0.5 stays
-0.5: a one-sided drain. Fix: `PackedNA.round_idx` (ties away from zero, odd map), used by
`refresh_scale_`, `quantize` and `scale_bytes_mse`. Regression test:
`tests/test_rescale_symmetry.py`.

CPU signal after the fix (d=64, 300 steps, seeds 0,1, `--rescale 10`, headroom, default
`scale_headroom=1.5`; before = same code with the old `bucketize` patched back in):

|---|---:|---|---:|---|

| na_e2m1_c5 | 3.4631 | -1:12.4% -0.5:22.6% +0:38.4% +0.5:16.1% +1:10.5% | 3.4407 | -1:11.8% -0.5:21.2% +0:33.7% +0.5:21.4% +1:11.9% |

## Conclusions

1. Fused bf16 Triton kernel: 1.2–4.4x faster than unfused decode+matmul and 2.3–7.6x
   faster than the fused fp32 mode (derived), but only 0.23–0.50x of dense bf16 GEMM.
2. `gap_mode="directional"` fixes the e2m1 instability (c5: mean 2.7510 → 2.4020, sd
   0.2221 → 0.0227) and costs wide_c3 (2.4307 → 2.5234).
3. Best quantized arms are 2.37–2.40 bits/char versus 2.033 dense: a ~0.34–0.37 gap.
4. `--rescale` with `scale_mode=headroom` was harmful on G4. One cause is fixed
   (tie rounding, above); a design problem remains (see "Open").

## Not verified / limits

- Single run per configuration (4 seeds, one machine). No repeat of the G4 session.
- Only the **forward GEMM kernel** was timed. End-to-end training-step speed
  (backward, update, pack/unpack) on sm_120 was **not** measured.
- Quality is a single tiny char-LM (d=128, 1500 steps) and one dataset.
- The G4 rescale runs (`rescale.log`) were produced **before** the tie-rounding fix; they
  were not re-run on GPU after it. The post-fix numbers above are CPU, small (d=64, 300
  steps, 2 seeds) and show symmetry only, not a quality verdict.
- `test_charlm` fails on both CPU and G4; its cause was not investigated here.

## Open

- With `scale_headroom=1.5` (the `MXNLinear` default) the refresh maps a block's max to
  ≤1.5·s, which on the wide ladder (max rung 3) and e2m1 (max rung 6) sits below the
  outer-rung threshold (midpoints 1.75 and 3.5). The outer rung is therefore unreachable
  right after any refresh, and a weight at ±3·s_old is pulled down to ±1·s_old. This is a
  headroom/ladder mismatch, not a sign bug. CPU control above: `scale_headroom` = max rung
keeps the outer rungs. Not yet done: re-run the G4 `--rescale` sweep with that headroom
(and the tie fix) to see whether periodic refresh helps quality at all.
